About Me

I'm Kiran, a Computer Science student who enjoys building things for the web — from small experiments to full-stack applications. I like taking an idea apart, understanding how it should work, and putting it back together as something people can actually use.

I'm most comfortable moving between the frontend and backend: designing interfaces that feel simple, and building the logic and data layers that keep them running. Outside of coursework, I spend my time on side projects, reading other people's code, and picking up new tools as I need them.

Right now, I'm focused on strengthening my fundamentals, contributing to real projects, and preparing for a software development internship where I can learn from experienced engineers.
